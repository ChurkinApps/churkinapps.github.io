Cig Runner Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

In short

  • There is no sign-up. Cig Runner doesn't ask for your name, e-mail, phone number, location, contacts or photos.
  • Coins, completed levels, records, the wardrobe and settings are stored only on your phone. They never reach me.
  • Level statistics and crash reports are sent to Yandex AppMetrica.
  • A Yandex ad is shown only when you choose to watch one for x10 coins. Ads are not personalised.

Who is responsible for the data

Alex Churkin (Чуркин Александр Сергеевич, ChurkinApps), the independent developer of Cig Runner. For statistics and crash reports I am the operator of personal data under Russian Federal Law No. 152-FZ and Law of the Republic of Belarus No. 99-Z "On Personal Data Protection". Contact: churkinapps@yandex.ru.

1. What is stored on the phone

Coins, unlocked and completed levels, stars, best times, secret coins found, items bought and worn in the wardrobe, settings (music and sound volume, vibration, language) and which tutorial hints have already been shown. All of it stays in the app's storage and is not sent anywhere. You can delete it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

2. Game statistics and crash reports

To see which levels are too hard and what breaks, the app sends to Yandex AppMetrica (Yandex LLC, servers in Russia):

  • the start of a level (its number) and a completed level: number, time, coins and stars collected;
  • how many times the hero died on a level;
  • wardrobe purchases for in-game coins: which item and its price in in-game coins (there is no real money in the game);
  • the offer to watch an ad and a watched ad for x10 coins;
  • game sessions (when the game was open and for how long);
  • crash reports: the error itself and the state of the app;
  • device details: model and manufacturer, Android and app versions, system language, connection type and mobile operator, installation identifier, IP address with the country and city derived from it, install source.

Location and the advertising identifier are not collected: the AppMetrica modules for them are not included in the app. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in keeping the game working and making it better.

3. Ads

After a level the game offers to watch a Yandex ad (Yandex Mobile Ads, Yandex LLC) to multiply the coins collected by 10. An ad is shown only if you tap that offer. Cig Runner tells the ad service that there is no consent to personalisation or location, so ads are not personalised. The app has no access to the advertising identifier: the permission for it is removed. To show an ad the service still needs the IP address, device model, system and app versions and impression data. Yandex processes this data under its own policy; it never reaches me.

4. Links

The links to this policy and to the list of third-party software open in your browser; from there the rules of the browser and the website apply.

Permissions

Internet and network state (ads and statistics), vibration (feedback on game events, can be turned off in the settings), install source from the app store (statistics and ads). The app has no access to files, contacts, the camera, the microphone or location.

Backups

If backup is turned on in Android, Android may save the game progress and settings to your account to move them to a new phone; this is done by Android and the phone maker.

Age

The hero of the game is a cigarette in a hat. There is no smoking, smoke or encouragement to smoke in the game, but because of this theme Cig Runner is not intended for children. I don't knowingly collect personal data of children. If you are a parent and believe your child's data has reached me, write to me and I will delete it.

How long the data is kept

On the phone, until you delete the app or its data. Statistics and crash reports are kept in Yandex AppMetrica for the period set by Yandex's terms. Everything is sent over encrypted connections.

Your rights

You can ask what data about you I have, ask me to correct or delete it, and object to its processing. Statistics don't contain names, so I can find your records by the approximate time of play and the device model. Uninstalling the app stops the sending. You may also complain to a data protection authority: in Russia, Roskomnadzor; in Belarus, the National Personal Data Protection Center.

Policies of the services

  • Google Play

Changes

A new version of the policy is published on this page with a new date.
